## In conclusion

* **Terraform** is the industry standard for declarative, multi-cloud **Infrastructure as Code (`IaC`)**.
* It bridges the gap between **Software Engineering** and **Cloud Operations** by bringing version control, peer reviews, and automated testing to infrastructure.
* Integrated into **CI/CD pipelines** (`GitOps`), `terraform plan` and `terraform apply` make cloud deployments predictable, auditable, and reproducible.
